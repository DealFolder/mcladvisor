How We Work

Fee Structure

Model A — Fixed Fee

Flat, scoped upfront. Best for defined engagements: diligence support, data room preparation, refi packages with a clear end point.

Model B — Fixed + Success Fee

Modest fixed fee through diligence, plus a negotiated percentage of purchase price charged to the transaction at close. Fixed fee credited at closing. Most common structure.

Model C — Monthly Retainer

Fixed monthly for ongoing pipeline coverage and portfolio oversight. Best for investors with active deal flow across multiple assets.

All engagements are fixed-fee and scoped in advance — no open-ended hourly billing. Fees for transaction engagements are structured to the deal and charged at closing. Pricing confirmed in the scoping call.

About

Michael Yousif

Michael Yousif has spent his career at the intersection of hotel investment, operations, and capital markets — advising on transactions, building portfolios, and negotiating the agreements that determine how assets perform long after the deal closes.

To him, hospitality has always been personal. It's the belief that where you stay shapes how you experience a place — that the right property at the right moment can make a trip feel like a memory worth keeping. He's drawn to hotels that create that feeling: places that are new and surprising, and places that feel like they've always been there.

Outside of work, Michael is a committed family man, a homemade pizza perfectionist, and a lifelong Beatles enthusiast. He rides bikes, lifts weights, spends time up north, and has taken up birding with the same curiosity he brings to everything he decides to learn.
