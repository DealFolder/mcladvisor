Technical Services & Pre-Opening
The Transition — Ground-Up Feasibility · Conversion · Pre-Opening Ramp
▼
  • Brand & Flag Strategy — Franchise evaluation, flag selection, PIP scope negotiation, and brand conversion planning.
  • Ground-Up Feasibility — Market and brand fit review during design and entitlement, ahead of construction.

03
Asset Advisory
The Hold — Performance · Operator Search · Capital Planning
▼
  • Performance Monitoring — RevPAR tracking, operator accountability, and NOI benchmarking against underwriting with clear escalation protocols.
  • Operator Search & Management Agreement Negotiation — Sourcing, RFP process, and fee/termination/key-man terms — for owners selecting an operator, or operators pursuing new contracts.
  • Capital Planning & CapEx Oversight — FF&E reserve strategy, PIP execution, and renovation scope management aligned to hold-period objectives.
  • Lender & Stakeholder Reporting — Investor and lender reporting packages, covenant compliance, and variance analysis.

About

Michael Yousif

Michael Yousif has spent his career at the intersection of hotel investment, operations, and capital markets — advising on transactions, building portfolios, and negotiating the agreements that determine how assets perform long after the deal closes.

To him, hospitality has always been personal. It's the belief that where you stay shapes how you experience a place — that the right property at the right moment can make a trip feel like a memory worth keeping. He's drawn to hotels that create that feeling: places that are new and surprising, and places that feel like they've always been there.

Outside of work, Michael is a committed family man, a homemade pizza perfectionist, and a lifelong Beatles enthusiast. He rides bikes, lifts weights, spends time up north, and has taken up birding with the same curiosity he brings to everything he decides to learn.
