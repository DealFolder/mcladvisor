What We Do

Four Service Areas

01

Transaction Advisory

Critical Path · Diligence · Disposition · Refinance · M&A · Capital Events

  • Critical Path & Diligence Management — Structuring the transaction timeline, coordinating all diligence workstreams, and keeping deals on track from LOI through close.
  • Disposition Preparation — Data room organization, asset documentation, sell-side positioning, and broker coordination to maximize price and speed to close.
  • Refinance & Recapitalization — Lender package preparation, DSCR covenant review, and capital structure optimization for refi and recap events.
  • M&A & Capital Events — Portfolio-level transaction support, JV structuring, and advisory through complex multi-asset or corporate-level deals.
02

Operator & Brand Selection

Management Search · Contract Negotiation · Flag Strategy

  • Operator Search & Evaluation — Sourcing, RFP process, and structured interview across full-service, select-service, and lifestyle segments.
  • Management Agreement Negotiation — Fee structures, performance thresholds, termination rights, and key-man provisions — aligned to owner outcomes.
  • Brand & Flag Strategy — Franchise evaluation, flag selection, PIP scope negotiation, and brand conversion planning.
  • Transition Management — Coordinating operator hand-off to protect NOI and guest continuity through ownership or management change.
03

Ongoing Investment Management

Portfolio Oversight · Performance Monitoring · Capital Planning

  • Portfolio Advisory Retainer — Ongoing coverage across active deal pipelines, asset reviews, and strategic decisions for owners managing multiple assets.
  • Performance Monitoring — RevPAR tracking, operator accountability, and NOI benchmarking against underwriting with clear escalation protocols.
  • Capital Planning & CapEx Oversight — FF&E reserve strategy, PIP execution, and renovation scope management aligned to hold-period objectives.
  • Lender & Stakeholder Reporting — Investor and lender reporting packages, covenant compliance, and variance analysis.
04

Asset Intelligence & Data

Document Abstraction · AI Analysis · Data Institutionalization

  • Contract & Document Abstraction — AI-powered extraction of key terms from management agreements, franchise agreements, loan documents, and ground leases.
  • Pre-Transaction Asset Documentation — Organize and institutionalize asset records before going to market — reducing buyer friction and supporting price.
  • Portfolio Data Infrastructure — Workflow design and data architecture for operators and owners managing 10–100+ assets.
  • AI Platform Advisory — Helping investors integrate AI into portfolio operations. Phase 1 assessment through ongoing implementation support.

How We Work

Fee Structure

Model A — Fixed Fee

Flat, scoped upfront. Best for defined engagements: diligence support, data room preparation, refi packages with a clear end point.

Model B — Fixed + Success Fee

Modest fixed fee through diligence, plus a negotiated percentage of purchase price charged to the transaction at close. Fixed fee credited at closing. Most common structure.

Model C — Monthly Retainer

Fixed monthly for ongoing pipeline coverage and portfolio oversight. Best for investors with active deal flow across multiple assets.

All engagements are fixed-fee and scoped in advance — no open-ended hourly billing. Fees for transaction engagements are structured to the deal and charged at closing. Pricing confirmed in the scoping call.

About

Michael Yousif

Michael Yousif has spent his career at the intersection of hotel investment, operations, and capital markets — advising on transactions, building portfolios, and negotiating the agreements that determine how assets perform long after the deal closes.

To him, hospitality has always been personal. It's the belief that where you stay shapes how you experience a place — that the right property at the right moment can make a trip feel like a memory worth keeping. He's drawn to hotels that create that feeling: places that are new and surprising, and places that feel like they've always been there.

Outside of work, Michael is a committed family man, a homemade pizza perfectionist, and a lifelong Beatles enthusiast. He rides bikes, lifts weights, spends time up north, and has taken up birding with the same curiosity he brings to everything he decides to learn.
