When you need a voice with no agenda.

In complex transactions, the most valuable advisor in the room is often the one who owes nothing to any party. MCL Advisors serves as an independent third-party opinion when the deal needs a credible, unconflicted read.

Dispute & Disagreement
When parties can't agree on value or viability

We provide independent operational and market analysis that both sides can reference — with full transparency on our methodology, so the conclusion is something both parties can stand on.

Lender vs. Borrower
Asset assessment in troubled situations

When a workout requires clarity on what the asset is actually worth — and what it would cost to fix — we deliver the honest read, grounded in operating reality rather than either side's position.

Multi-Party Transactions
Brands, investors, and operators at the same table

We help complex deals move forward by providing a shared, expert frame of reference no single party could credibly provide on their own.

How We Approach It

Independence isn't a tagline — it's a structure.

A few principles guide every third-party or mediation-style engagement.

No Mandate to Sell
We don't represent a brokerage, lender, or brand. Our conclusions aren't shaped by a transaction we need to close.
Transparent Methodology
Every assessment shows its work — assumptions, comp sets, and data sources are disclosed so all parties can evaluate the reasoning, not just the conclusion.
Operator-Grounded
Our read comes from having run the operational side of hotel deals, not just modeled them — which is what makes the opinion credible to both finance and operations people.
Scoped Engagement
We agree on scope, access, and deliverable format upfront — with both parties, where applicable — so the process itself isn't a point of contention.
